# About Me: Kimberly Menchaca Napoles

Hello everyone! My name is Kimberly Menchaca Napoles, and I am a Chemistry major with a concentration in Biochemistry. I am currently working toward my degree and expect to graduate in 2028. I chose Chemistry because I enjoy learning about how things work at a molecular level and how chemistry connects to other areas of science and medicine.

## My Goals

One of my biggest goals in life is to earn my master’s degree in Chemistry and then continue my education by attending medical school. My ultimate goal is to work in the medical field as an OB-GYN doctor. I am interested in becoming an OB-GYN because I want to help women throughout different stages of their lives and make a positive difference in the lives of my future patients. I know that reaching this goal will take a lot of hard work and dedication, but I am excited about the journey and everything I will learn along the way.

## My Hobbies

When I am not studying, I enjoy spending my free time doing things that allow me to relax and have fun. One of my hobbies is crocheting, which I enjoy because it allows me to be creative and make something with my own hands. I also enjoy going on walks, especially when I want to clear my mind and take a break from school. Another thing I enjoy is trying new things. I like having new experiences, learning about different activities, and stepping outside of my comfort zone.

## My Favorite Subjects in College

My favorite subjects in college are chemistry and biology. I enjoy chemistry because it allows me to understand how substances interact and how things work at the molecular level. I also enjoy biology because I like learning about living organisms, the human body, and how different biological systems work together. Both subjects are especially interesting to me because they connect to my future goal of attending medical school and becoming an OB-GYN.

## Looking Ahead

Overall, I am excited about my college journey and everything that the future has in store for me. I know that becoming a doctor will require many years of education and hard work, but it is a goal that I am passionate about. I hope to continue growing academically and personally while working toward my dream career in medicine.
